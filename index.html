About Dinosaurs

Dinosaurs were a diverse group of reptiles that dominated the Earth for over 160 million years. From the massive Brachiosaurus to the fearsome Tyrannosaurus Rex, these creatures shaped our planet's history.

Featured Dinosaurs

Tyrannosaurus Rex

Late Cretaceous

The king of the dinosaurs, T-Rex was one of the largest meat-eating dinosaurs ever to roam the Earth.

Triceratops

Late Cretaceous

A massive herbivore with three horns, known for its defensive capabilities and impressive frilled head.

Stegosaurus

Dinosaurs: Still With Us Today

Did you know that dinosaurs didn't completely disappear? Birds are actually living descendants of theropod dinosaurs! This means you can see dinosaur genetics alive today in chickens, eagles, and pigeons.

Modern Animal Connections

  • Birds: Direct descendants of small theropod dinosaurs - sharing similar bone structures and behaviors
  • Crocodiles: Part of the same archosaur group as dinosaurs, making them distant cousins
  • Lizards & Snakes: Share common reptilian ancestry with dinosaurs

Scientists have found that modern birds inherited many traits from their dinosaur ancestors, including feathers, egg-laying, and skeletal structures. Every time you see a bird, you're looking at a living dinosaur!
